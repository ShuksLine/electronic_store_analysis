#ELECTRONIC PRODUCT PERFORMANCE AND STOCK LEVEL OVERVIEW

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This project focuses on analyzing electronic store data to understand sales performance, product trends and stock level overview. The analysis aims to identify useful patterns and generate insights that can support better business decisions.

OBJECTIVE:
In this project I answered the following questions:
1. Examining overall sales performance
2. Identifying top-performing products and categories
3. Identify underperforming products
4. Product availability and turnover
5. Market and Customer Preferences
6. Provide insights to support business growth

TOOLS I USED:
Excel (PivotTable, Functions & Formula, VLOOKUP)

KEY ACTIVITIES:
1. Data inspection and cleaning
2. Data exploration and analysis
3. Identification of product sales pattern and trends
4. Product stock-level count
5. Presentation of findings and recommendations.

PROJECT OUTCOME:
The analysis provides insights into product performance, helping the business better understand its operation and make informed decisions.

KEY INSIGHT:
1. Found that Home appliances has best selling products
2. Found that Home appliances generated 75% revenue in the business
3. Found that Vehicles has the lowest selling rate
5. Found top 10 available and best selling products
6. Found Customer preferences in product selection/purchase
